Straight answers

What dealers ask first.

How does the dealership get paid on finance?

Carki pays commission on every completed finance agreement. Their own pitch to dealers is that they will not be beaten on it, and the schedule is agreed with you when you onboard rather than published here, because it depends on the size and mix of your forecourt.

The customer pays no fee to Carki for the broking, and the commission does not change the rate they are offered or the total they repay. Ask for the current schedule in writing before you sign anything.

Is the 20-second decision a real application?

It is a soft-search eligibility check, so the customer gets a decision in principle without a mark being left on their credit file. A full application and credit check comes later, when they have chosen a car and want to proceed.

Who is regulated here, and who is not?

Carki is a trading style of Equaliti Ltd, authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, FRN 952416, and is a credit broker rather than a lender. All regulated finance wording, rates and representative examples are signed off by Carki before they appear.

Digital Sales Consultants Ltd is not authorised by the FCA and does not arrange credit. We build and run the website and the marketing, and we check published copy against the wording Carki has approved.

What does the digital side cost?

From £120 a month for a 30-car forecourt, including the stock system, the website and a directory network listing. 80 cars from £170, 250 cars from £369. Setup included.

A bigger site or a bespoke design is quoted per dealer, in writing, before any work starts.

Can we keep our current finance partner?

Yes. The website, the stock system and the directory listings stand on their own. You would not be earning commission on those deals, and you would not get the stock advertising, but nothing breaks.

Do you fund our stock?

No, and we will not imply otherwise. Carki arranges finance for the person buying the car, not funding for the forecourt's own stock. They are different products, and mixing them in a pitch is how dealers end up with the wrong agreement.

What happens to our domain, stock and photos?

They come with you. The domain is normally transferred in from the previous provider, the stock feed is re-pointed rather than rebuilt, and nobody re-uploads a car by hand. Existing photos are reused, and the listing checks flag the ones that are missing or wrong.

Who do we ring when something is wrong?

1 number, WhatsApp or call. A car wrong on a directory listing, a page that will not load, a finance figure that looks off on a vehicle page: it goes to the same team, and it is the team that built it.

Straight answers

What dealers ask first.

Who is regulated here, and who is not?

Forward Motor Finance Ltd is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, FRN 1031643, and is a credit broker rather than a lender. All regulated finance wording, rates and representative examples are signed off by Forward Motor Finance before they appear.

Digital Sales Consultants Ltd is not authorised by the FCA and does not arrange credit. We build and run the website and the marketing, and we check published copy against the wording Forward Motor Finance has approved.

What does it cost to offer finance through you?

Nothing to the dealership and nothing to the customer. Forward Motor Finance is paid by the lender for arranging the agreement, either as a fixed fee or a percentage of the amount borrowed.

The commission your dealership earns on an agreement, where commission applies, is agreed with Forward Motor Finance when you are approved. Ask for it in writing before you sign anything rather than taking a figure from a page.

Do you take customers with poor credit?

Yes. All credit histories are considered, including customers who have been refused elsewhere, but every application is still subject to status and affordability. Nobody here can promise an approval before the application has been looked at.

What does the digital side cost?

From £120 a month for a 30-car forecourt, including the stock system, the website and a directory network listing. 80 cars from £170, 250 cars from £369. Setup included.

A bigger site or a bespoke design is quoted per dealer, in writing, before any work starts.

Do you fund our stock?

No, and we will not imply otherwise. Forward Motor Finance arranges finance for the person buying the car, not funding for the forecourt's own stock. Those are different products, and mixing them in a pitch is how dealers end up with the wrong agreement. Asset and business finance is a separate conversation, arranged through Forward Business Finance.

Can we keep our current finance partner?

Yes. The website, the stock system and the directory listings stand on their own. You would not have the panel behind those deals and you would not get the free dealer page, but nothing breaks.

What happens to our domain, stock and photos?

They come with you. The domain is normally transferred in from the previous provider, the stock feed is re-pointed rather than rebuilt, and nobody re-uploads a car by hand. Existing photos are reused, and the listing checks flag the ones that are missing or wrong.

Who do we ring when something is wrong?

1 number, WhatsApp or call. A car wrong on a directory listing, a page that will not load, a finance figure that looks off on a vehicle page: it goes to the same team, and it is the team that built it.

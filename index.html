Worth being straight

What it is not.

A platform pitch that lists everything the competition lists is worth nothing. Here is the edge of ours.

Not a DMS

We feed stock out of the stock system or DMS you already run, or quote Autosales alongside it. We would rather connect to what works than ask you to run 2 systems.

Not stock funding

Carki arranges customer finance, not forecourt funding. Stocking a forecourt is a different product from financing the person buying the car, and we will not blur the two.

Not a valuation tool

Pricing comes from the Auto Trader data you already pay for. Our job is making sure every listing on every channel agrees with it.

Straight answers

Questions dealers actually ask.

What does the whole thing cost?

From £120 a month for a 30-car forecourt, including the stock system, the website and a directory network listing. 80 cars from £170, 250 cars from £369. No VAT, setup included, finance at no fee to you.

A bigger site or a bespoke design is quoted per dealer, in writing, before any work starts. There is no range published because no 2 forecourts need the same build.

How quickly can we be live?

The date is agreed with the figure, not after it. A standard site on an existing stock feed is days of work, not months. Where the domain and feed have to be moved from another provider, that move is scheduled into the same date and the old site stays up until the new one is serving.

Do I have to leave my current website provider or DMS?

No. There are 3 usual routes. If you own the site and hosting, we rebuild in place and nothing moves. If your site sits on a provider's platform with no code to hand over, it is rebuilt on ours, and the domain, feed and redirects come with it. If you are mid-contract, we build now and go live the day the notice ends, so nobody pays for 2 sites.

Your DMS stays where it is. We connect to it or feed from it.

Who is regulated here, and who is not?

Carki is a trading style of Equaliti Ltd, authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, FRN 952416, and is a credit broker rather than a lender. All regulated finance wording, rates and representative examples are signed off by Carki before they appear.

Digital Sales Consultants Ltd is not authorised by the FCA and does not arrange credit. We build and run the website and the marketing, and we check published copy against the wording Carki has approved.

Can you fund my stock?

No, and we will not imply otherwise. Carki arranges finance for the person buying the car, not funding for the forecourt's own stock. They are different products, and mixing them in a pitch is how dealers end up with the wrong agreement.

What happens to my domain, my stock and my photos?

They come with you. The domain is normally transferred in from the previous provider, the stock feed is re-pointed rather than rebuilt, and nobody re-uploads a car by hand. Photos already on the feed are reused, and the listing checks flag the ones that are missing or wrong rather than quietly publishing a placeholder.

Do I have to take the finance to get the website?

No. The website, the stock system and the directory listings stand on their own. Plenty of dealers start with the site and add Carki later, and some never use Carki at all. If you already have a finance partner you are happy with, keep them, though you will not be earning commission on those deals.

How does the dealership get paid on finance?

Carki pays commission on every completed finance agreement. Carki's own pitch to dealers is that they will not be beaten on it, and the schedule is agreed with you when you onboard rather than published here, because it depends on the size and mix of your forecourt.

The customer pays no fee to Carki for the broking, and the commission does not change the rate they are offered or the total they repay. Ask us for the current schedule in writing before you sign anything.

Who do I ring when something is wrong?

1 number, WhatsApp or call. A car wrong on a directory listing, a page that will not load, a finance figure that looks off on a vehicle page: it goes to the same team, and it is the team that built it.
